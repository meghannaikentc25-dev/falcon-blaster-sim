Where the motion comes from

Start with the mouse, then use a phone's gyroscope as a stand-in IMU, then plug in the real ESP32 and this page becomes the test bench.

Aim with the mouse (or drag on touch). Everything downstream is simulated from that motion.
Serial protocol 
ESP32 → PC, 100 Hz, 115200 baud:
$gx,gy,gz,ax,ay,az,yaw,pitch,roll,T,B1,B2,B3,JX,JY
  gyro °/s (x fwd, y left, z up) · accel g · fused angles °
  T trigger · B1 reload · B2 jump · B3 weapon · joystick −1…1

PC → ESP32:
  Z  re-centre yaw      C  calibrate gyro
  V<0-255> motor       L<0|1> muzzle LED
  B<hz>,<ms> buzzer     H<zone> hit pattern
  A<n> ammo LEDs        R  reload chirp

Power budget

ESP32 with BLE ≈ 120 mA · MPU6050 4 mA · LEDs 6 × 10 mA · motor 70 mA (bursts) · buzzer 20 mA → ~200 mA average, ~350 mA peak. A 2600 mAh 18650 gives roughly 8 h; through an MT3608 boost at 5 V the budget is still comfortable.
